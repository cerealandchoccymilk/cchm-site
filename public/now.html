School

I'm finally attending a public college after going to community college for the past few years.
My major is in Computer Science, but I'm enjoying my multicultural gender studies class so much that a Gender Studies or Anthropology minor is looking very appealing right now....

Tech

As part of my transition to a new college, I installed Linux Mint onto my frying pan of a MacBook Air. It used to heat up easily and struggle with opening any kind of app on MacOS, but now it's so fast quiet, it's amazing.
The installation itself was a bit troublesome, but thankfully I found the t2linux wiki which helped me get my drivers working correctly.

I've moved from Notion to Obsidian for my writing and note-taking workflow.
I was a bit skeptical since I had tried other markdown editors and didn't like them, but this ended up being perfect for me. Thank goodness for the git integration plugin (and the loads of other plugins I've installed).

Language

Primarily working on my 17776 Japanese translation project.
Progress is unfortunately slow due to college, but I'm pushing through it and doing what I can. Major thanks to my collaborators, as always.

Doujin activity

My last doujin submission for the Marx × Gryll Anthology (still in stock as of writing!) turned out great! Although I couldn't attend the event in person as planned, it was nice to be able to be part of it in that way.

I'm now chipping away at my manga submission for another anthology I've joined, due next year. It will be my first manga ever so I'm pretty nervous, but I'll try my best. Also considering joining yet another anthology, but I have to gauge if it's actually feasible.

Personal

My partner broke up with me (on good terms). I've decided to focus on my friends and community for at least a while, rather than follow the urge to find romantic love again.
I currently spend my weekends with my friends drinking and hanging out at local craft events, when my schedule allows.
