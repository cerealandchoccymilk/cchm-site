Akuma Kun After-Air Radio / 『悪魔くん』アフターラジオ

この番組は、あたかも地上波でオンエアしているシリーズアニメのごとく毎回1つのエピソードについてキャストとスタッフが楽しく語るラジオです。
A podcast where the cast and staff of the Netflix Akuma-Kun have fun talking about each episode, released once per week as if it were a TV-broadcast anime series.
(taken from video description)

From 2023/11/9 to 2024/1/25, the official Akuma-Kun Twitter hosted a Japan-wide watch party. Every Thursday at 10PM, Akuma-Kun fans, including some of the cast and staff, would (re-)watch one episode of the show and tweet live comments under the hashtag #悪魔くん同時視聴 ("Akuma-Kun simultaneous viewing"). At 10:30PM, the After-Air Radio for the corresponsing episode would be released on YouTube for a limited time, providing behind-the-scenes commentary.

I have archived all episodes, and am in the process of translating the contents. I will only post the English translations here, but the videos (and maybe the Japanese transcriptions) will also be kept personally, just in case.
