手塚治虫作「ブラック・ジャック」　28話『指』（封印回）
Links
Notion mirror / Complete Mangadex scanlation
Info
Unlike many other of the sealed chapters, this chapter was sealed not because of direct controversy but because Tezuka was not happy with how it turned out (however, the depiction of people with polydactyly may also have been a factor). It got remade into 『刻印』“Imprint,” which was included in later collections and Vertical’s volume 5.
