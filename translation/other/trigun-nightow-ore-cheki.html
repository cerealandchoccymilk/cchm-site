Noob-mangaka Nightows "Ore-cheki" A-to-Z

Noob-mangaka Nightow's "Ore-cheki" A-to-Z

Y’know. I’m a pretty worn-out otaku. I’ve seen a lot. So I was under the impression that I wouldn’t be fazed by most things…

But when I was shown this concept art, I nearly died then and there!!
He shot right through my weak point without any hesitation; he might as well be called a king.

Man, Yoshimatsu-san scares me… in many ways.

Translator's note

I'm not sure what "ore-cheki" means. Ore is "me/my" and cheki is a name for Instax cameras (and photos taken with it). Cheki could also mean "check-it?"

There is a bit of context missing, but basically Nightow liked the twins' redesigns a lot, and this is him reacting in sort of in a "they're too cute!!" way.
